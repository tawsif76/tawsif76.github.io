01 / Background

A little
about me.

I recently graduated in Computer Science and Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology, with a strong interest in computer networking and security. My work spans traditional TCP/IP-based systems and future Internet architectures such as Named Data Networking (NDN).

I am currently working on Vehicular NDN, where I aim to design an efficient push-based mechanism for disseminating critical data. I work with C/C++ and Python, network simulation tools including ns-3 and ndnSIM, and mobility simulation with SUMO.

I am seeking Research Assistantship positions in networking and security, particularly vehicular networks, cyber-physical systems, IoT, blockchain, NDN, and 5G/6G-inspired architectures.
