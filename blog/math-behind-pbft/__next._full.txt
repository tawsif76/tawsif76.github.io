<p>PBFT (Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance) is a widely used consensus algorithm in systems where performance and fault tolerance are both important. It is commonly used in permissioned blockchains to ensure that multiple nodes can agree on the same data, even if some of the nodes are faulty or malicious. But we often ruminate over one question:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Why does PBFT require a minimum of <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>3</mn><mi>f</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">3f + 1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">3</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span> total nodes, and why does it need <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>2</mn><mi>f</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">2f + 1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">2</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span> votes to commit a decision?</p>
</blockquote>
<h2>The Two Core Problems to Solve</h2>
<p>To be considered reliable, any consensus algorithm must solve two essential problems:</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p>The system must never finalize two conflicting decisions. For example, it should not be possible for both Block A and Block B to be committed at the same height. Once a decision is made, it is final.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The system must continue making progress. Even if some nodes are faulty or unresponsive, the honest nodes should still be able to reach consensus and not get stuck.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>The rules <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi><mo>≥</mo><mn>3</mn><mi>f</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n \geq 3f + 1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.7719em;vertical-align:-0.136em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">≥</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">3</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span> (total nodes) and <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>2</mn><mi>f</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q = 2f + 1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.625em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span> (quorum size) are the minimum requirements needed to satisfy both properties.</p>
<h2>Step-by-Step Derivation</h2>
<p>Let's define our terms first:</p>
<ul>
<li><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span></span></span></span>: The maximum number of nodes that can be faulty</li>
<li><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.4306em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span></span></span></span>: The total number of nodes in the system</li>
<li><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.625em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span></span></span></span>: The number of votes needed to commit a value (the quorum size)</li>
</ul>
<p>We will derive the values of <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.625em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span></span></span></span> and <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.4306em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span></span></span></span> from our two goals above, showing how they are logically connected.</p>
<h3>1. Quorum Size (q) from the Safety Condition</h3>
<p>To ensure safety, we must prevent two conflicting decisions from being committed at the same time. For that, any two quorums must share at least one honest node.</p>
<p>This overlap guarantees that an honest node will witness both decisions. Since honest nodes follow the protocol and never vote for two different values at the same height, the existence of an honest node in the overlap ensures safety.</p>
<p>Let's say two quorums of size <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.625em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span></span></span></span> form: Quorum A and Quorum B. The minimum number of shared nodes between them is:</p>
<div style="text-align: center; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><menclose notation="box"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="false"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="false"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="true"><mrow><mtext>Overlap</mtext><mo>≥</mo><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mi>q</mi><mo>+</mo><mi>q</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mo>−</mo><mi>n</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>2</mn><mi>q</mi><mo>−</mo><mi>n</mi></mrow></mstyle></mstyle></mstyle></menclose></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\boxed{\text{Overlap} \geq (q + q) - n = 2q - n}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.68em;vertical-align:-0.59em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:1.09em;"><span style="top:-3.68em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3.68em;"></span><span class="boxpad"><span class="mord"><span class="mord"><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">Overlap</span></span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">≥</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mopen">(</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span><span class="mclose">)</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">−</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mord">2</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">−</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span></span></span></span></span><span style="top:-3.09em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3.68em;"></span><span class="stretchy fbox" style="height:1.68em;border-style:solid;border-width:0.04em;"></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.59em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>
</div>
<p>To be safe, this overlap must contain more nodes than the total number of faulty nodes, <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span></span></span></span>. This gives us the <strong>Safety Condition</strong>:</p>
<div style="text-align: center; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><menclose notation="box"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="false"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="false"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="true"><mrow><mn>2</mn><mi>q</mi><mo>−</mo><mi>n</mi><mo>></mo><mi>f</mi></mrow></mstyle></mstyle></mstyle></menclose></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\boxed{2q - n > f}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.5689em;vertical-align:-0.5344em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:1.0344em;"><span style="top:-3.5689em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3.5689em;"></span><span class="boxpad"><span class="mord"><span class="mord"><span class="mord">2</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">−</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">></span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span></span></span></span></span><span style="top:-3.0344em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3.5689em;"></span><span class="stretchy fbox" style="height:1.5689em;border-style:solid;border-width:0.04em;"></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.5344em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>
</div>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>The Marble Analogy: Why the Overlap Must Be f + 1</strong></p>
<p>To understand why the overlap must be greater than <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span></span></span></span>, consider this analogy:</p>
<p>Imagine a bag with red marbles (faulty nodes) and blue marbles (honest nodes). You are told there are at most 5 red marbles (<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>5</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f = 5</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">5</span></span></span></span>). How many marbles must you draw to be absolutely certain you have at least one blue marble?</p>
<p>In the worst-case scenario, you might first draw all 5 red marbles. Only on the 6th draw (<span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f + 1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span>) can you be sure the marble is blue.</p>
<p>This is why a quorum overlap must be at least <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f + 1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span>—to guarantee at least one honest node is included.</p>
</blockquote>

<h3>2. Total Nodes (n) from the Liveness Condition</h3>
<p>To ensure liveness, the system must make progress even if all <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span></span></span></span> faulty nodes stop responding. In this worst case, the remaining honest nodes must still be able to form a quorum by themselves.</p>
<ul>
<li>The number of honest nodes is <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi><mo>−</mo><mi>f</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n - f</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6667em;vertical-align:-0.0833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">−</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span></span></span></span></li>
<li>The required quorum size is <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.625em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span></span></span></span></li>
</ul>
<p>This gives us the <strong>Liveness Condition</strong>:</p>
<div style="text-align: center; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><menclose notation="box"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="false"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="false"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="true"><mrow><mi>n</mi><mo>−</mo><mi>f</mi><mo>≥</mo><mi>q</mi></mrow></mstyle></mstyle></mstyle></menclose></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\boxed{n - f \geq q}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.5689em;vertical-align:-0.5344em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:1.0344em;"><span style="top:-3.5689em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3.5689em;"></span><span class="boxpad"><span class="mord"><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">−</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">≥</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span></span></span></span></span><span style="top:-3.0344em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3.5689em;"></span><span class="stretchy fbox" style="height:1.5689em;border-style:solid;border-width:0.04em;"></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.5344em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p>Substitute this into the safety condition:</p>

<p>The smallest integer <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.625em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span></span></span></span> that satisfies this is:</p>

<p>So, we have logically derived both:</p>
<ul>
<li>The number of votes required to commit a value is <strong><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>2</mn><mi>f</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">2f + 1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">2</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span></strong></li>
<li>The total number of nodes must be at least <strong><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>3</mn><mi>f</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">3f + 1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">3</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span></strong></li>
</ul>

<h2>An Example Walkthrough</h2>
<p>Let's say the system is designed to tolerate <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>4</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f = 4</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">4</span></span></span></span> faulty nodes.</p>
<p><strong>Total Nodes Needed:</strong></p>
<div style="text-align: center; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>3</mn><mo>×</mo><mn>4</mn><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>=</mo><mn>13</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n = 3 \times 4 + 1 = 13</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.4306em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.7278em;vertical-align:-0.0833em;"></span><span class="mord">3</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">×</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.7278em;vertical-align:-0.0833em;"></span><span class="mord">4</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">13</span></span></span></span></p>
</div>
<p><strong>Quorum Size:</strong></p>
<div style="text-align: center; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>q</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>2</mn><mo>×</mo><mn>4</mn><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>=</mo><mn>9</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">q = 2 \times 4 + 1 = 9</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.625em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">q</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.7278em;vertical-align:-0.0833em;"></span><span class="mord">2</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">×</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.7278em;vertical-align:-0.0833em;"></span><span class="mord">4</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">9</span></span></span></span></p>
</div>
<p>Now, suppose an attacker tries to get two different blocks committed: Block A and Block B.</p>
<ul>
<li>Quorum A (9 nodes) votes for Block A</li>
<li>Quorum B (9 nodes) votes for Block B</li>
</ul>
<p>Since there are only 13 nodes in total, the minimum overlap is:</p>
<div style="text-align: center; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
<p><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><menclose notation="box"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="false"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="false"><mstyle scriptlevel="0" displaystyle="true"><mrow><mtext>Overlap</mtext><mo>=</mo><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mn>2</mn><mo>×</mo><mn>9</mn><mo stretchy="false">)</mo><mo>−</mo><mn>13</mn><mo>=</mo><mn>5</mn></mrow></mstyle></mstyle></mstyle></menclose></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\boxed{\text{Overlap} = (2 \times 9) - 13 = 5}</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:1.68em;vertical-align:-0.59em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="vlist-t vlist-t2"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:1.09em;"><span style="top:-3.68em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3.68em;"></span><span class="boxpad"><span class="mord"><span class="mord"><span class="mord text"><span class="mord">Overlap</span></span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mopen">(</span><span class="mord">2</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">×</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mord">9</span><span class="mclose">)</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">−</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mord">13</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mord">5</span></span></span></span></span><span style="top:-3.09em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:3.68em;"></span><span class="stretchy fbox" style="height:1.68em;border-style:solid;border-width:0.04em;"></span></span></span><span class="vlist-s">​</span></span><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.59em;"><span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>
